# Traditional Chinese: style and terms

How gym-app reads in 中文 (zh-Hant-TW). Every Chinese entry in `web/src/i18n/zh/` follows this page.

## Voice

- Taiwan usage, the words people say in a Taiwan gym. Not mainland terms: 影片 not 視頻, 螢幕 not 屏幕, 資訊 not 信息, 設定 not 設置, 儲存 not 保存.
- 你, never 您. Short and plain, like the English: commands stay commands (開始訓練, not 請點選開始訓練).
- No exclamation marks unless the English has one.

## Punctuation and spacing

- Full-width punctuation inside Chinese sentences: ，。：；？！（）「」、
- A space between Chinese and a number or Latin word: `第 3 組`, `約 52 分鐘`, `本週 4 組`, `用 RPE 記錄`.
- No space inside a number and its unit when the English has none: `100kg`, `+2.5kg/週`.
- `×` stays: `3 × 8`. Ranges use `–`: `8–10 次`.
- Quotes are 「」.
- The `// ` note prefix stays as is.
- Dates: `11月23日 週一`, with year `2026年11月23日`, month header `2026年11月`. Weekday initials `一 二 三 四 五 六 日` (Monday first).

## Kept in Latin

kg, PR, 1RM, e1RM, RPE, RIR, CSV, PWA, `EN`.
